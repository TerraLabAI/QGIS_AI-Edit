about=
    Show a project before it exists, on the aerial imagery you already have open

    • Select an area, write what you want, get the result back as a georeferenced layer on the same extent and CRS as your source imagery, tagged as AI-generated

    • The most used case: an orthophoto redrawn as a clean illustrative site plan. Then redevelopments, planted streets, densified blocks, parks, solar farms, flood and sea level scenarios, enhanced aerials, clouds and cars removed, land cover drawn as flat colours

    • Works on any raster on your map: GeoTIFF, JPG, PNG, and online basemaps (WMS, XYZ tiles). Vector layers and other rasters can guide the result as references

    • Guide the result: reference images or layers, a sketch on the map, iterations, and a before/after slider

    • Vectorize: a generated map with flat colours becomes editable polygons, exported to GeoPackage, Shapefile or GeoJSON

    • 100+ ready-made prompts, ranked by what people actually run, or write your own

    • What it is not: a preview, not a document. No dimensions, no legal value, not a site plan for a permit. Not a detector: for building, tree or parcel outlines with real geometry, use AI Segmentation. Not a prediction: a flood scenario is an illustration

    • Windows, macOS, Linux, no GPU needed. Free tier without a card. Powered by Google's Nano Banana image model, run on TerraLab servers
